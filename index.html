Key Findings & Insights

High Attack Volume: 39% of network traffic classified as potential attacks (8,727 out of 22,544 events), indicating significant threat exposure across the monitored network infrastructure.
Model Performance: Logistic Regression model achieved 75.4% accuracy with exceptional 91.8% precision, minimizing false positives. Training completed in just 4.69 seconds, demonstrating efficiency for real-time deployment.
TCP Dominance: TCP protocol accounts for the majority of intrusion attempts (11,755 high-risk events out of 18,880 total TCP traffic), highlighting the need for enhanced TCP traffic monitoring and filtering.
Service-Level Targeting: HTTP service alone accounts for 7,853 attack attempts, followed by telnet (1,626) and private services (1,400), suggesting targeted exploitation of web and remote access protocols.
Critical Risk Priority: 59.76% of traffic (13,472 events) flagged as high-risk requires immediate attention. The model's high precision ensures that security teams can trust alerts and prioritize responses effectively.
Protocol Security Profile: While TCP and UDP show high attack rates (62-64% of their traffic), ICMP remains predominantly safe (93.8% normal), indicating attackers primarily target connection-oriented protocols.
